# 📊 ApnoMart Sales Performance Dashboard

### The Insight Board | ApnoAI Data Analytics Internship

## 🔹 Project Overview
The **ApnoMart Business Intelligence Dashboard** is an interactive sales analysis project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It provides a clear overview of sales, profit, orders, and profitability to help identify business trends and support data-driven decisions.

## 🛠️ Tool Used
- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop**
## 📈 Dashboard Features

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Total Sales: 16.26M

Total Profit: 4.42M

Total Orders: Approximately 1,200

Profit Margin: 27.18%

Total Quantity: 6,736

# Visualizations

Sales by Category: Compares sales performance across product categories.

Monthly Sales Trend: Tracks changes in sales over time.

Profit by Region: Compares profitability across regions.

Interactive Slicers: Allow users to filter the report by month, region, and category.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

**1. Sales Performance by Category**  
Sales vary across product categories. Identifying the strongest-performing categories can help ApnoMart improve inventory management and focus marketing efforts on products with greater sales potential.

**2. Regional Profitability**  
Profitability differs across regions. Comparing regional performance can help identify opportunities to review costs, discounts, and product mix to improve profit.

**3. Customer Purchasing Behavior**  
Customer segments and payment methods provide useful information about purchasing patterns. These insights can help ApnoMart improve customer engagement and develop more targeted marketing strategies.

## 💡 Business Recommendation
Monitor sales and profit trends regularly, maintain appropriate stock levels for strong-performing categories, and investigate regional differences to support sustainable and profitable growth.
